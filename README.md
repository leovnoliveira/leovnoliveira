### Hey there! 👋

I'm **Leonardo**, a Data & AI Engineer based in Brazil.

I put LLMs into production and build the data pipelines behind them, from ingestion to evaluation.

At **Quaest**, I built an AI platform that audits survey interviews with speech-to-text and LLMs.

My main stack is Python, SQL, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Docker and AWS/GCP.

My favorite side project is [Plateia](https://github.com/leovnoliveira/plateia), an agent that tells you what your YouTube audience asks, wants and complains about.

I also contribute to [Base dos Dados](https://github.com/basedosdados), Brazil's open public-data platform.

**Fun facts:**
- 📈 Economist by training, so I like to measure whether things actually work
- 🏆 Best Undergraduate Thesis in Economics award (2019)
- 🌎 Portuguese, English, and a bit of Spanish and French
